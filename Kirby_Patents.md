# Kirby Vacuum Patents

Every **US vacuum-cleaner-specific patent** by **James B. Kirby** (1884–1971),
sorted by **application (filing) date**. Utility + design patents; design
marked **(D)**.

**Source:** Google Patents — inventor "James B. Kirby," filtered to `country=US`
+ the vacuum-cleaning classification (**CPC A47L**: catches *vacuum / suction /
pneumatic / sweeper*, not just "vacuum") and title-verified. Assignees: *Kirby* =
himself; *Barnett* = Harley B. Barnett (his early backer); *EVC Co* = Electric
Vacuum Cleaner Co; *III* = Industrial Improvements Inc — all his inventions,
just assigned.

**PDFs:** the patent PDFs live in `patents/assets/US<PATENT>.pdf`. To (re)fetch
them use [`tools/patents-from-json.py`](tools/patents-from-json.py) (browser-saved
JSON method — Google bot-blocks curl) or [`tools/fetch-patents.sh`](tools/fetch-patents.sh)
where curl works; both write to `patents/assets/` and skip files already there.

## Notes

- **Excluded** (not vacuum-specific): his large laundry / washing-machine,
  dishwasher, and ironing output, plus the outliers — shock absorbers, a radio
  condenser, a **fishing reel**, and a patent literally titled **"Lake or
  pond."**
- **Name caveat:** a couple of 1960s "tooth-cleaning device" patents appear
  under the same name — not vacuum, and likely a namesake. His vacuum work ends
  at the 1953 filings.
- **[US1182092](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1182092A)** ("Lubricating
  device") is kept despite the generic title: Kirby's spec says it was designed
  for "the shafts of high speed motors such as are employed in electrically
  operated vacuum cleaning machines" — i.e., the cleaner's motor-shaft bearing.
  Google files it under **B61F15/14** (railway axle-box bearings), so both a
  keyword search and the CPC A47L filter miss it — only the spec text catches it.
  (A reminder that other dual-use Kirby patents may hide under non-cleaning classes.)
- **[US2648396](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2648396A)** ("Vacuum
  cleaner," granted 1953-08-11) is the 1953 patent worth framing for the wall.
- This file can become the site's **Patents page** (sortable table + local PDF
  links), alongside the models list and manuals.
